A few answers first

Where does the .cube work?
Anywhere that reads a 3D LUT: DaVinci Resolve, Premiere, Final Cut, After Effects, OBS, most game engines, and a good number of cameras.

My regions did not come through in the .cube.
They cannot. A 3D LUT maps one colour to another and has no way to know where in the frame a pixel sits, so nothing built from a mask can travel inside one. The export button says global only when a look has regions. To get the regions, export the photograph, or run the folder through Apply to photos.

Do I need an account?
Only for Lucy. Grading, masks, RAW development, batch export and writing a .cube all happen on your Mac and need nobody's permission.
