Privacy

What leaves your Mac, and when.

Last updated 10 August 2026

Your photographs

Luminara does not copy your photos. A project stores a reference to the file where it already lives, and reads it when you open the project.

Photos stay on your Mac while you grade by hand. Everything the controls do happens on your machine, including masks, RAW development and baking the .cube. A photo leaves only when you ask Lucy something, and only then.

Asking Lucy

When you ask Lucy for something, these travel to Luminara's server and on to the model service that answers:

  • what you typed
  • the look's current settings, as numbers
  • measurements of the photograph
  • the photograph itself, and any reference image you attach

Luminara's server does not store the contents of your conversations or your photographs. It records one row per turn, so your weekly allowance can be counted and so failures can be told apart from successes:

  • which tier answered, and which week it belongs to
  • whether the turn succeeded, failed, or is still running
  • how many tokens went up and came back
  • how long the model service took, and the status it returned
  • when the turn started and finished

None of that includes what you typed or what was in the picture.

Your account

Signing in stores your email address, or the identifier Apple gives us if you use Sign in with Apple, along with a note of whether the address is one of Apple's forwarding addresses. Sign-in codes are sent by email and expire after ten minutes.

Alongside it we keep which plan you hold, when it expires, and the identifiers from the App Store receipt that prove it. Sign-in codes and session tokens are never stored as themselves, only as their SHA-256, so a copy of the database is a list of who has an account rather than a way to be any of them.

Subscriptions are handled by the App Store. Luminara receives the receipt Apple signs, which says which subscription you hold and when it expires. We never see your payment details.

How long it is kept

  • Sign-in codes: deleted an hour after they expire, or as soon as they are used.
  • Rate-limit counters: deleted after a day.
  • Session tokens: until you sign out or delete the account.
  • Your account and its usage rows: until you delete the account.

Deleting the account removes all of it in one step, including every usage row.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no sale of anything to anyone.
  • No third-party analytics.
  • Nothing is collected from you before you sign in.

Deleting your account

In the app: Settings, then Account, then Delete account. It takes effect at once. Your address, your sign-in tokens and every usage record go with it.

Or write to luminara.feedback@hotmail.com and ask. Projects and looks live on your Mac and are yours to delete. An App Store subscription is not cancelled by deleting the account; cancel it in App Store settings.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what leaves your Mac, the date above changes and the new terms appear here before they take effect.
