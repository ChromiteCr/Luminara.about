Privacy

What leaves your Mac, and when.

Last updated 8 August 2026

Your photographs

Luminara does not copy your photos. A project stores a reference to the file where it already lives, and reads it when you open the project.

Photos stay on your Mac while you grade by hand. Everything the controls do happens on your machine, including masks, RAW development and baking the .cube. A photo leaves only when you ask Lucy something, and only then.

Asking Lucy

When you ask Lucy for something, these travel to Luminara's server and on to the model service that answers:

  • what you typed
  • the look's current settings, as numbers
  • measurements of the photograph
  • the photograph itself, and any reference image you attach

Luminara's server records that a turn happened, which tier it used and how many tokens it cost, so allowances can be counted. It does not store the contents of your conversations or your photographs.

Your account

Signing in stores your email address, or the identifier Apple gives us if you use Sign in with Apple. Sign-in codes are sent by email and expire after ten minutes.

Subscriptions are handled by the App Store. Luminara receives the receipt Apple signs, which says which subscription you hold and when it expires. We never see your payment details.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no sale of anything to anyone.
  • No third-party analytics.
  • Nothing is collected from you before you sign in.

Deleting your account

Write to luminara.feedback@hotmail.com and your account and its usage records are removed. Projects and looks live on your Mac and are yours to delete.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what leaves your Mac, the date above changes and the new terms appear here before they take effect.
