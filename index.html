Local adjustments

A region is a stack you can argue with.

Compose it out of parts: a brush, a gradient, a colour qualifier, the subject the photo is of. Add, subtract and intersect them.

One click builds a sky, and it is not a sky detector. It is three ordinary parts, all still on screen and all still editable.

  • Gradient from the top add
  • Blues & cyans intersect
  • Subject subtract
  • Blur 12 · Opacity 100% refine

A folder at a time

Every region is solved again on each photo.

A region built from a rule rather than a shape gets re-solved on every picture it meets, so a sky is that photo's sky. Eight frames are previewed before the run starts.

The report says, per photo, which rules found something and which did not. A rule that found nothing is named rather than skipped quietly.

Two speeds

One answers quickly. The other reads you better.

Chroma is for the turns where you already know what you want and just want it done. Spectra is slower and better at working out what you meant, which is the one to reach for when the request is vague or the picture is awkward.
